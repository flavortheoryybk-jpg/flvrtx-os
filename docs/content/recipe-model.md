# Recipe Content Model

The Recipe content type is the foundation of FLVRTX.

Every published recipe should educate, inspire, and guide users to cook with confidence.

---

# Purpose

Recipe pages should provide:

- Clear instructions
- Ingredient lists
- Cooking techniques
- Supporting videos
- Related learning resources
- Product recommendations

---

# Content Structure

## Hero

- Title
- Featured Image
- Short Description
- Prep Time
- Cook Time
- Servings
- Difficulty

---

## Ingredients

Include:

- Exact quantities
- Units
- Ingredient names

Example:

- 500g Chicken
- 2 tbsp Oil
- 1 Onion

---

## Instructions

Use short, actionable steps.

Example:

1. Heat oil.
2. Add onions.
3. Cook until golden.
4. Add spices.

Avoid long paragraphs.

---

## Equipment

List only equipment required.

Examples:

- Pressure Cooker
- Cast Iron Pan
- Blender

---

## Chef Tips

Helpful cooking advice.

Examples:

- Don't overcook onions.
- Rest meat before serving.
- Toast spices first.

---

## Storage

Include:

- Refrigerator duration
- Freezer duration
- Reheating instructions

---

## FAQ

Answer common questions.

Examples:

- Can I make it vegetarian?
- Can I freeze it?
- Can I cook it without a pressure cooker?

---

## Video

Link the YouTube recipe.

---

## Internal Links

Each recipe should connect to:

- Learn Article
- Watch Video
- Product Recommendation
- Related Recipes

---

# SEO Requirements

Every recipe should include:

- SEO Title
- Meta Description
- Featured Image
- Alt Text
- Recipe Schema

---

# Images

Required:

- Hero Image

Recommended:

- Process Images
- Final Dish

---

# Publishing Checklist

Before publishing:

- Title completed
- Featured image added
- Ingredients verified
- Instructions reviewed
- Video linked
- Related recipes added
- Learn article linked
- Product recommendation linked
- SEO completed
- Schema validated

---

# Goal

Every recipe should provide a complete cooking experience rather than just a list of ingredients and instructions.
