# Watch Content Model

The Watch content type connects FLVRTX recipes with video content.

Every Watch page should provide a premium video viewing experience while encouraging users to explore the full recipe and related educational content.

---

# Purpose

Watch pages should:

- Showcase the cooking video
- Support the written recipe
- Increase YouTube engagement
- Improve user experience
- Connect users with related FLVRTX content

---

# Content Structure

## Hero

Include:

- Video Title
- Featured Thumbnail
- Short Description

---

## Video

Embed the YouTube video using the official YouTube URL.

Always use responsive embeds.

---

## Description

Provide a short written summary of:

- What users will learn
- Cooking highlights
- Tips covered in the video

---

## Related Recipe

Every Watch page should link to its corresponding Recipe.

---

## Related Learn Articles

Link educational articles that explain techniques or ingredients featured in the video.

---

## Related Recommendations

Recommend useful kitchen tools, ingredients, or products shown in the video.

---

# Video Guidelines

Videos should:

- Be high quality
- Match the written recipe
- Have a descriptive title
- Include a custom thumbnail
- Use consistent branding

---

# Images

Required:

- Featured Thumbnail

Optional:

- Behind-the-scenes images
- Finished dish

---

# Internal Linking

Each Watch page should connect to:

- Recipe
- Learn Article
- Product Recommendation
- Related Videos

---

# SEO Requirements

Every Watch page should include:

- SEO Title
- Meta Description
- Featured Image
- Alt Text
- Embedded Video

---

# Publishing Checklist

Before publishing:

- Title completed
- Featured thumbnail added
- YouTube video embedded
- Description reviewed
- Related recipe linked
- Learn article linked
- Recommendation linked
- SEO completed

---

# Goal

Transform every cooking video into a complete learning experience that encourages users to cook, learn, and explore more content across FLVRTX.
