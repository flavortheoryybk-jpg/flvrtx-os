# Recommendation Content Model

The Recommendation content type helps users discover trusted products that improve their cooking experience.

Recommendations should be honest, practical, and based on real-world usage whenever possible.

---

# Purpose

Recommendation pages should:

- Help users choose the right product
- Explain why the product is recommended
- Highlight strengths and limitations
- Connect products to recipes
- Support affiliate monetization transparently

---

# Content Structure

## Hero

Include:

- Product Name
- Product Image
- Short Summary
- Rating
- Price (if applicable)
- Product Category

---

## Product Overview

Explain:

- What the product is
- Who it is for
- Why it is recommended

---

## Why We Recommend It

Provide a detailed explanation based on:

- Performance
- Ease of use
- Value for money
- Durability
- Cooking experience

---

## Pros

List the main advantages.

Examples:

- Durable
- Easy to clean
- Excellent heat retention

---

## Cons

List honest limitations.

Examples:

- Heavy
- Premium price
- Requires maintenance

---

## Best For

Recommend the ideal audience.

Examples:

- Beginners
- Home cooks
- Professional chefs
- Meal prep

---

## Purchase Links

Supported platforms:

- Amazon
- Flipkart
- Meesho

Affiliate links should always include the appropriate disclosure.

---

## Related Recipes

Show recipes where the product is used.

---

## Related Learn Articles

Link educational content explaining the cooking techniques or ingredients associated with the product.

---

# Review Guidelines

Recommendations should be:

- Honest
- Transparent
- Practical
- Experience-based

Avoid exaggerated marketing language.

---

# Affiliate Disclosure

Clearly state when affiliate links are used.

Example:

"This page may contain affiliate links. If you purchase through these links, FLVRTX may earn a small commission at no additional cost to you."

---

# SEO Requirements

Every recommendation page should include:

- SEO Title
- Meta Description
- Featured Image
- Alt Text

---

# Publishing Checklist

Before publishing:

- Product information verified
- Featured image added
- Pros and cons reviewed
- Purchase links tested
- Affiliate disclosure added
- Related recipes linked
- Learn articles linked
- SEO completed

---

# Goal

Recommend products that genuinely help people cook better while maintaining trust, transparency, and long-term credibility.
