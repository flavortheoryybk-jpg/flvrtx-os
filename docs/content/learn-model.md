# Learn Content Model

The Learn content type helps users understand the science, techniques, ingredients, and culture behind cooking.

The goal is to educate readers so they become better home cooks.

---

# Purpose

Learn articles should answer:

- Why something works
- How something works
- Best practices
- Common mistakes
- Food science
- Ingredient knowledge

---

# Content Structure

## Hero

- Title
- Featured Image
- Short Introduction

---

## Article Body

The main educational content.

Write in clear, simple language.

Break content into logical sections.

---

## Key Takeaways

Summarize the most important learning points.

Use short bullet points.

---

## Did You Know

Share one interesting fact related to the topic.

---

## Related Recipes

Link recipes where the concept is applied.

---

## Related Articles

Link other Learn articles.

---

# Writing Style

Articles should be:

- Educational
- Practical
- Easy to understand
- Backed by reliable sources where appropriate

Avoid unnecessary jargon.

---

# Images

Use images only when they improve understanding.

Examples:

- Ingredient comparison
- Cooking technique
- Process illustration

---

# Internal Linking

Each Learn article should link to:

- Related Recipes
- Related Learn Articles
- Watch Videos
- Product Recommendations (when relevant)

---

# SEO Requirements

Every article should include:

- SEO Title
- Meta Description
- Featured Image
- Alt Text

---

# Publishing Checklist

Before publishing:

- Title completed
- Featured image added
- Introduction written
- Article reviewed
- Key Takeaways added
- Did You Know added
- Related Recipes linked
- Related Articles linked
- SEO completed

---

# Goal

Help readers understand food, not just follow recipes.
